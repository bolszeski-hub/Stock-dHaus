Storage and thawing

Move everything to the freezer as soon as it arrives, still sealed. Vacuum-sealed cuts keep their best quality for up to [12] months at 0°F.

Thaw in the fridge, still sealed, for 24 to 48 hours; large cuts may need up to 72 hours. Never thaw on the counter. For a quicker thaw, submerge the sealed package in cold water and change the water every 30 minutes.
